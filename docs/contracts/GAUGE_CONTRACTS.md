# Gauge contracts (generated)

Generated by `scripts/gen_gauge_contract_docs.py` from `src/app/application/gauge/contracts.py`.
Do not edit by hand: change the contract, then regenerate. CI fails when this file is stale.

Schema version: `datamesh-gauge/v1`. Results are `embedding_geometry`: a lattice-gauge reading of reduced embeddings,
not an operational forecast. Gauges are read-only measurements and are not GNN inputs.

Layering: Pydantic only. Arrays live in `app.infrastructure.gauge` and the shared `InMemoryNumpyTensorStore`;
contracts carry `TensorRef` handles. `TensorContract`, `NodeRole`, `TensorSpec` and `TensorRef` are the simulation slice's own classes.

## Gauge vs measurement

Gauge-dependent (change under a frame rotation, never measurements): `director`, `edge_connection`.
Gauge-invariant (measurements): `node_gauges`, `node_mask`, `edge_index`, `edge_link`, `edge_reliability`, `triangles`, `triangle_holonomy`, `triangle_charge`, `triangle_z2`, and `loops`.

## Operand gauge features

Column order of `GaugeResult.node_gauges` (axis `feature`):

| # | Feature | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | `planarity` | lambda2 / lambda3 of the local patch (capped at 1e6): how well a 2-D tangent plane fits. Local. |
| 1 | `anisotropy` | lambda1 / lambda2 (capped at 1e6): how well defined the principal-axis director is. Local. |
| 2 | `log_density_ratio` | log(global median kNN spacing / local kNN spacing). > 0 denser than the cloud. Local vs global. |
| 3 | `supersaturated` | 1 when exp(log_density_ratio) >= supersaturation_threshold, else 0. Local vs global. |
| 4 | `link_coherence` | Mean cos(m * link) over incident links (m = 2 axis, 1 vector): local alignment order. Local. |
| 5 | `coherence_vs_global` | link_coherence minus the cloud-wide mean link coherence. Local vs global. |
| 6 | `net_holonomy` | Sum of incident triangle holonomies (radians): local curvature of the connection. |
| 7 | `defect_load` | Sum of |charge| over incident triangles: vortex (defect) load on the operand. |
| 8 | `z2_defects` | Incident triangles whose frames cannot be oriented consistently (z2 = -1). |
| 9 | `degree` | Links kept in the connection (reliable, orientable, both ends valid). |
