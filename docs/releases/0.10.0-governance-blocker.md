## Governance foundation required by G03

These are review requirements for a versioned governance contract, not new
runtime fields, approved business vocabulary or permissions.

| Concern | Decision and implementation acceptance |
| --- | --- |
| Ownership and stewardship | Identify accountable business owner, steward and policy owner for each governed data class/product. Keep ownership distinct from a source-reported owner or an executor role. |
| Classification and access scope | Associate sensitivity and organization/project/customer scope with exact record/artifact revisions. Define unknown handling and deny unconfigured disclosure. |
| Permitted use and source rights | Record authorized processing, external services, training and publication separately; access to a document is not permission to reuse or publish it. |
| Derived information | Preserve exact inputs and transformation/profile/model versions. Derived graphs, rankings, embeddings, projections and metrics inherit all applicable source restrictions until an authorized disclosure decision changes the permitted output. Opaque IDs and hashes are not declassification. |
| Policy identity and history | Pin governance-policy versions independently of schema/record versions. Preserve effective time, recorded decisions, corrections and review history without silently relabeling old captures. |
| Metrics and commercial meaning | Name the measure owner, population, exclusions, units, time basis and rule version. Keep estimates, deployed capacity, recorded labor, sell value, ordered commitments and actual incurred costs distinct. |
| Disclosure and distribution | Record who authorized an exact output/version, for which audience and purpose, with evidence. Work release, document receipt and successful publication do not grant disclosure rights. |
| Access enforcement | Before broader enterprise access, implement attributable principals and project/data-class authorization across reads, graph traversal, exports and background processing. Shared-token possession and private-LAN placement are insufficient. |
| Retention, holds and disposition | Define policies for source blobs, histories, graphs, embeddings, broker data, exports and backups. Connect eventual disposition to retaining references, holds and independent authority; append-only storage is not a retention policy. |

Existing [VersionedEntity](../../src/app/domain/versioning.py) and ingestion
[DomainObject](../../src/app/application/ingest/contracts.py) capture identity,
versions and provenance, but do not provide a common classification/ownership
contract. DataProduct, DataLineage, DistributionEvent and MetricDefinition in
`src/app/domain/data_governance/` are unreviewed scaffolds, not implemented
enterprise-wide controls. Reuse existing evidence/versioning mechanisms rather
than adding competing authority or lineage models.

## Alpha boundary and later implementation

0.10.0 retains local shared-token access, privacy-projected reads and the
existing documented limits. G03 must place enforceable implementation gates
before broader enterprise access, unrestricted ingestion/derivation, sensitive
reporting or deployment that relies on those controls. An approved plan does
not mean the controls exist.

Preserve observed/inferred/unknown distinctions, independent reference
verification, historical restore without fresh-verification claims, exact
canonical versions, separate contract/subcontract meanings and blocked
operational release. No data-governance scaffold is promoted to an accepted
definition here. Identity-provider integration, runtime authorization,
classification propagation and physical deletion are not implemented by this PR.
